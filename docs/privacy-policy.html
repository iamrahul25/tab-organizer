Tab Organizer Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Summary

Tab Organizer works entirely inside your browser. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your data with anyone.

What the extension accesses

To do its job, Tab Organizer reads the titles, addresses (URLs), and icons of your open tabs and tab groups. It uses this only to sort tabs, close duplicates, group tabs, suspend inactive tabs, show the vertical tabs side panel, and save and restore tab sessions that you choose to save.

What is stored

Your settings (sort choice, theme, display size, automatic grouping) and the sessions you save are stored in your browser using Chrome's storage. If Chrome sync is turned on, Chrome may sync this data between your own devices through your Google account. We do not receive or have access to it.

What is not done

  • No data is sent to any server owned by us or anyone else.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No data is sold or transferred to third parties.
  • No remote code is loaded. All code is included in the extension package.

Your control

You can delete any saved session from the extension at any time. Removing the extension deletes its local data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
